# Vision

## One-liner

**MostWanted.** "Jailbreak, but Minecraft." Guards vs. prisoners in one persistent open world. Break out, rob the city, and stay ahead of the guards. Or be the guard who brings them back.

## Inspiration

Games, servers, shows, and maps worth looking at:

- **Roblox Jailbreak**: the main reference. Guards vs. prisoners, escape → rob → get arrested loop, persistent world
- Prison Architect / The Escapists (crafting tools, routines, escape routes)
- Prison Break (the show): planning, tattoos-as-maps, inside help
- Cops and Robbers style Minecraft maps
- Classic Minecraft "Prison" servers (mine → rank up). Different genre, but worth knowing what players expect when they hear "prison"
