# Gameplay

## The big choice: format

| Option | How it works | Pros | Cons |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Round-based** | Matches of ~20–40 min. Prisoners try to escape, guards try to stop them. Reset after. | Easy to balance, fresh every game, fits Minehut's sleep-when-empty servers | Needs enough players online at once |
| **Persistent** | One long-running prison. You serve a "sentence," escape, get caught, get thrown back in. | Deep progression, players log in daily | Harder to balance, harder to build, economy upkeep |
| **Hybrid** | Persistent progression (cosmetics, perks, stats) with round-based escapes | Best of both | Most work |

## Roles

- **Prisoners**: the main role. Daily routine (roll call, meals, work, lockdown), with chances to sneak off and work on an escape.
- **Guards**: players or NPCs? Player guards are more fun but need staffing/population. NPC guards work with low player counts.
- **Warden**: special role, maybe a top guard or staff?
- **Snitches / informants**: prisoners who can sell out other prisoners?

## Daily routine (idea)

A short in-game "day" cycle that structures play:

1. Roll call: must be at your cell / the yard or you get flagged
2. Breakfast / mess hall
3. Work duty: kitchen, laundry, workshop, which give access to materials
4. Free time / yard
5. Lockdown: back in cells, the riskiest time to be out

## Escape routes (idea)

Multiple ways out so every round plays differently:

- Dig a tunnel (spoon → shovel progression, hide the hole behind a poster)
- Steal a guard uniform / keycard and walk out
- Vents
- Start a riot as a distraction
- Bribe a guard
- Get out via the laundry truck / delivery

## Contraband & crafting

- Items found or smuggled during work duty
- Combine into tools (file, rope, fake ID, makeshift shovel)
- Cell searches by guards; hiding spots

## Win / lose

- Prisoners win by escaping (solo or as a crew?)
- Guards win by preventing escapes / catching everyone
- Getting caught → solitary confinement, lose items, longer sentence
