# Getting help

- **Playing on the server:** ask in the server's Discord, or open a ticket at [mostwantedmc.com/support](https://mostwantedmc.com/support).
- **Bugs and ideas:** [open an issue](https://github.com/xena-studios/mostwanted/issues/new/choose). For a bug, include the version from `/mostwanted info`, what you did, and any console errors.
- **Security problems:** follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), never a public issue.
- **Anything else:** [contact@xenastudios.co](mailto:contact@xenastudios.co).
