MostWanted is a new Minecraft server from [Xena Studios](https://xenastudios.co): cops vs. prisoners, in one desert world that never resets.

## What it is

Everyone starts as an **inmate**. Break out through a weak wall, the sewers or a stolen keycard, and you become a **criminal**: rob the bank, the jewelry store and the gas station, and get the cash to a hideout before the **guards** bring you back. Guards are picked by application, and they collect the bounty on everyone they arrest.

It runs on **Java and Bedrock**, through Minehut.

## What's next

- The map: the prison, the town and the desert around them.
- The game itself: escapes, robberies, arrests, bounties, riots and custom gear.
- A playtest before launch.

Everything is built in the open. The code and the design docs are [on GitHub](https://github.com/xena-studios/mostwanted).
