Guards are picked by [application](/apply). Everyone starts as an inmate.

## What guards do

- Patrol the prison and stop escapes.
- Answer robbery alarms and bring criminals in.
- Hold the prison during a riot.

## Tools

- **Handcuffs:** arrest a criminal at close range.
- **Taser:** slows a target for a moment, on a cooldown.
- **The armory:** guns and gear, bought with your pay and bounties.

## Pay

Guards earn a salary while on duty, and the **bounty** of every criminal they arrest.

## On and off duty

Guards can go off duty whenever they like and play as an inmate.

## The guard code

- Never help criminals: no letting friends go, no tip-offs, no passing gear.
- Only arrest or taser players who are escaping, rioting or wanted.
- No camping safe zones or spawns.

Breaking the code costs you the rank. See the [rules](/rules).
