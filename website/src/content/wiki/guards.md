Guards are picked by [application](/apply). Everyone starts as an inmate. If you're accepted, you get the guard rank, and with it the `/duty` command.

## On and off duty

- Type `/duty` as an inmate to go **on duty**. You're sent to the police station with your gear.
- Type `/duty` again to go **off duty**. You're an inmate again, back in the prison.
- You can't go off duty within **15 seconds** of a fight.
- You can't go on duty as a criminal, or while serving a sentence.
- Guard money is kept apart from your inmate money. Each switch swaps between them.

## Your gear

You get this on duty. You can't drop it, and you get it back when you respawn.

- **Handcuffs:** right-click a criminal within **3 blocks** to arrest them. One click, so it works the same on Bedrock.
- **Taser:** no damage, but slows a criminal right down for 3 seconds so you can cuff them. Recharges in 10 seconds. See [Guns and ammo](/wiki/guns-and-ammo).
- **Keycard:** opens gates. You can't hand it over, but when you die there's a **50% chance** it drops, and inmates can use it to escape.

Guns, ammo, armor and food are at the **armory** in the police station, at half the hideout price. Use `/shop` there. See [Money and shops](/wiki/money-and-shops).

## Arresting

| How | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| Cuff a criminal | Their whole bounty |
| Kill a criminal | Half their bounty |

Cuffing pays double, so it's worth getting close. You can't cuff a criminal in a hideout or a safe zone, but you can cuff one who walks into the police station.

During a riot you can also cuff inmates inside the prison. They have no bounty, so that pays nothing, but catching every rioter ends the riot. See [Riots](/wiki/riots).

Your scoreboard counts your arrests and the bounty you've earned.

## Alerts

Every guard on duty is told when:

- A **robbery** starts, with an alarm sound. See [Robberies and bounties](/wiki/robberies).
- A **prison wall** is broken, and which one.
- An inmate **uses a keycard**, with their name and the gate.
- A **riot** starts, with a horn.

## Dying

Criminals can fight back. If one kills you, they get **+$500 bounty**. You come back at the police station with your gear.

## The guard code

- Never help criminals: no letting friends go, no tip-offs, no passing gear.
- Only arrest or taser players who are escaping, rioting or wanted.
- No camping safe zones or spawns.

Breaking the code costs you the rank. See the [rules](/rules).
