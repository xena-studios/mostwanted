## Robbery spots

| Location | Risk | Payout |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Gas station** | Low | Small, quick. Good for your first job. |
| **Jewelry store** | Medium | Lasers and tripwires to get past. |
| **Bank** | High | The biggest payout, a long timer, and an alarm every guard hears. |
| **Big heists** | Very high | Rotating events: the train, the museum, the cargo ship. |

Each spot **opens and closes** on a timer, and the server announces it. When the bank opens, everyone knows, including the guards.

## Bounties

Every robbery adds to your **bounty**. The guard who arrests you collects it. The highest bounties show on the **Most Wanted** board, in game and on the [leaderboards](/leaderboards).

## Keeping your cash

Cash you're carrying is **unbanked**. Get it to a **criminal hideout** to bank it. Get arrested first and it's gone.
