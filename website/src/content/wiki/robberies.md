## How a robbery works

1. Find a robbery spot that's **open**, like the bank.
2. A criminal walks in, and the robbery **starts**. Every guard on duty hears the **alarm** and is told where.
3. Stay inside until the time is up. A **red bar** at the top of your screen fills as you go.
4. Make it to the end and you're paid. If every robber leaves or gets caught, the robbery **fails**.
5. Either way, the spot **closes** for a while. When it opens again, the server tells everyone.

While a robbery is on, everyone's scoreboard shows a red **HEIST** section: the place, the time left, and how many guards are on duty.

Only criminals can rob. You need to be out of the prison first. See [Escaping](/wiki/escaping).

## What it pays

These are the current numbers. They can change as we balance the game.

| Spot | Time inside | Payout | Bounty for each robber | Closed after |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Bank** | 2 minutes | $10,000 | $5,000 | 15 minutes |
| **Other spots** | 1 minute | $2,500 | $1,500 | 10 minutes |

- **The payout is split** between every criminal inside when the time runs out. Leave early and you get nothing.
- **More guards, more money.** The payout goes up **10% for every guard on duty**, up to **double**. With 3 guards on, a $2,500 robbery pays $3,250.
- Every robber who makes it gets the **full bounty**, not a share.

## Bounties

Your **bounty** is the price on your head.

- Each robbery adds to it.
- Killing a guard on duty adds **$500**.

Your scoreboard shows your bounty and your **wanted stars**:

| Bounty | Stars |
| --- | --- |
| Any bounty | ★ |
| $1,000 | ★★ |
| $5,000 | ★★★ |
| $15,000 | ★★★★ |
| $50,000 | ★★★★★ |

The guard who arrests you collects your bounty, and a bigger bounty means a longer sentence. An arrest resets your bounty to zero. See [Arrests and jail](/wiki/arrests-and-jail). The highest bounties also show on the [leaderboards](/leaderboards).

## Keeping your cash

Robbery money goes into your **cash**. Cash you're carrying is **unbanked**: get arrested and it's gone. Walk into a **criminal hideout** and it's banked for you, safe for good. See [Money and shops](/wiki/money-and-shops).
