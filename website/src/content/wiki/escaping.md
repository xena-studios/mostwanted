There's more than one way out, and the guards know the obvious ones.

## Ways out

### Punch through a weak wall

Some parts of the prison wall are weak. Punch the **same block 20 times in a row**, with no more than **1.5 seconds** between punches. No tool needed. Cracks show how close you are. Stop for too long and the count starts again.

- When the block breaks, **every guard on duty is told** which wall it was.
- The hole **grows back after 2 minutes**. It waits if someone is standing in it.

### Use a keycard on a gate

Gates are locked. Right-click one while holding a **keycard** and it opens for **5 seconds**. It won't close on someone standing in it.

- Guards carry a keycard on duty. When a guard dies, there's a **50% chance** it drops.
- Using a keycard tells **every guard on duty** your name and which gate you opened.
- Guards open gates without a keycard.

### The vents and sewers

Some routes are part of the map, like vents and sewers. Look for wooden trapdoors you can open by hand.

### A riot

When enough inmates riot, the guards are busy in the yard. See [Riots](/wiki/riots).

### Help from outside

Criminals can punch weak walls from the outside too, so a friend can break you out. And anyone but a guard can drop a keycard for someone else.

## Once you're out

Get outside the prison and you've **escaped**. You become a **criminal**, and the whole server is told.

Guards can now fight you and arrest you. Head for town to rob something, or for a **hideout** to bank your cash and stay safe. See [Robberies and bounties](/wiki/robberies).

## Getting caught

While you're still inside the prison, guards can't hurt or arrest you, unless there's a riot. They can still see the alerts and wait for you outside.

Once you're a criminal, getting arrested sends you to a cell, then back to the prison. You lose your unbanked cash, your guns and ammo, and any keycard. Your bank is safe. See [Arrests and jail](/wiki/arrests-and-jail).

You can't break walls, open gates or press the riot alarm while you're serving a sentence.
