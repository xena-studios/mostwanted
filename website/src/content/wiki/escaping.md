There's more than one way out, and the guards know the obvious ones.

## Ways out

- **Break a weak wall or fence.** Needs the right tool, and time.
- **The sewers and vents.** Quiet, but guards patrol the exits.
- **A keycard.** Guards drop them, and some turn up during work duty. Keycards open gates.
- **Over the wall.** With a ladder or a grapple.
- **A riot.** When the prison is in chaos, nobody's watching the gates. See [Riots](/wiki/riots).

## Once you're out

Cross the prison boundary and you become a **criminal**. Guards can now arrest you anywhere. Head for town, or for a hideout to lie low.

## Getting caught

Arrested on the way out? Back to your cell. You keep what you've banked; you lose what you were carrying.
