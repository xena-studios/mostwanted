A riot takes several inmates working together. One person can't start one alone.

## Starting a riot

The **riot alarm** is in the prison yard. Walking into it tells you so.

- Stand in the alarm and **right-click**. That presses it.
- **3 inmates** need to press it within **30 seconds** of each other.
- At least **1 guard** has to be on duty. No riot with nobody to riot against.
- Riots have a **30-minute cooldown**, counted from when the last one ended.
- You can't press it while you're serving a sentence.

These are the current numbers. They can change as we balance the game.

## During a riot

- A riot lasts up to **5 minutes**.
- Inmates and guards can **fight inside the prison**. Safe zones still count.
- Guards can **cuff inmates** inside the prison. That's a **1-minute** sentence, and like any arrest it takes unbanked cash, guns, ammo and keycards.
- The whole server is told. Guards hear a horn and are called back to the prison.
- Everyone's scoreboard shows a red **RIOT** section: how many rioters, the time left, and how many guards are on duty.

The **rioters** are the inmates who pressed the alarm, plus any inmate who hits a guard during the riot.

With the guards busy at the prison, a riot is good cover for an [escape](/wiki/escaping), or for a [robbery](/wiki/robberies) in town.

## How it ends

- **Time runs out** after 5 minutes, or
- **The guards stop it:** every rioter has been arrested, has escaped, or has left the game.

Then the 30-minute cooldown starts.
