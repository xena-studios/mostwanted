A riot takes several inmates working together. One person can't start one alone.

## During a riot

- Inmates and guards can **fight inside the prison**.
- Every guard is alerted, and the ones in town come running.
- That makes it the best cover there is for an escape, or a robbery in town.

## How it ends

The riot ends on a timer, when guards retake control, or when most rioters are down or arrested. Then there's a cooldown before the next one.
