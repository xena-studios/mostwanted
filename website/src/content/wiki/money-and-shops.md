## Cash and bank

Your money is in two places. Both show on your scoreboard.

- **Cash** is what you're carrying. Robberies and arrests pay into it.
- **Bank** is safe. An arrest never touches it.

## Banking

As a criminal, walk into a **hideout** and all your cash is **banked for you**. There's nothing to click.

Until then it's **unbanked**. Get arrested, or die, and you lose all of it. So after a robbery, get to a hideout before the guards get to you.

## Guard money

Guards keep their own cash and bank, apart from their inmate money. Going on or off duty with `/duty` swaps between the two. Guards are never arrested, so they never lose their cash.

## Shops

Shops are chest menus, the same on Java and Bedrock. Open one with `/shop`, or by clicking the shopkeeper.

| Shop | Where | Who |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Hideout shop** | Criminal hideouts | Criminals |
| **Armory** | The police station | Guards on duty |

- Prices come out of your **cash first, then your bank**.
- The gold ingot in the corner shows your cash and bank.
- If your inventory is full, you aren't charged.
- You can't shop while you're serving a sentence.
