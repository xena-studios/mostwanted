## Who can hurt who

| | Guard | Criminal | Inmate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Guard** | No | Yes | Only while they're escaping, or in a riot |
| **Criminal** | Yes | No direct damage | No |
| **Inmate** | Only in a riot | No | No |

Criminals can't hit each other directly, but there are other ways to get ahead: traps, tipping off the guards, and sabotage.

## Guns

Pistols, shotguns and rifles, bought with cash. They work the same on Java and Bedrock. Ammo costs money too.

## Dying

A criminal killed by a guard counts as **arrested**: back to prison, and the guard collects the bounty.

## Safe zones

No fighting in the police station, the criminal hideouts, or the cell blocks outside a riot.
