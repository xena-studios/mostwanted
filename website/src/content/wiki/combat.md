## Who can hurt who

| | Guard | Criminal | Inmate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Guard** | No | Yes | Only in a riot, inside the prison |
| **Criminal** | Yes | No | No |
| **Inmate** | Only in a riot, inside the prison | No | No |

Guards and criminals fight. Nobody else does, unless there's a [riot](/wiki/riots). This goes for fists, guns, and anything else you can hit with.

## Dying

- **A criminal who dies is arrested.** If a guard killed them, the guard gets half their bounty. If they died any other way, nobody gets paid. See [Arrests and jail](/wiki/arrests-and-jail).
- **A criminal who kills a guard on duty** gets **+$500 bounty**.
- **A guard who dies** comes back at the police station with their gear. Their keycard has a 50% chance to drop.
- **An inmate who dies** comes back in the prison.

## Safe zones

| Place | Who's safe |
| --- | --- |
| **Safe zones** | Everyone. No fighting at all. |
| **The police station** | Guards |
| **Criminal hideouts** | Criminals |
| **A cell, while serving a sentence** | The inmate. Nothing hurts them. |

If you're safe in a zone, you can't attack from it either. A guard can't shoot out of the police station, and a criminal can't shoot out of a hideout.

Guards can still **cuff** a criminal who walks into the police station.

## Guards and fights

A guard who has just been in a fight can't go off duty for **15 seconds**.

## Guns

Guns follow the same rules: a shot at someone you can't hurt does nothing. See [Guns and ammo](/wiki/guns-and-ammo).
