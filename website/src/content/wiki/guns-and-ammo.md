## Shooting

- **Right-click** to shoot.
- Shots are **instant**: they land along where you're looking, the moment you fire. It works the same on Java and Bedrock.
- A hit on the **head** does **1.5×** damage.
- Guns follow the [combat rules](/wiki/combat). A shot at someone you can't hurt, or into a safe zone, does nothing.

## Reloading

- Each gun keeps its own rounds. A new gun comes fully loaded.
- When it's empty, it **reloads by itself** from the ammo in your inventory. There's no reload button.
- Switching to another item stops the reload.
- Holding a gun shows its ammo above your hotbar, like `Pistol 7/12 · 36 spare`.
- Out of ammo? Buy more at a hideout or the armory. See [Money and shops](/wiki/money-and-shops).

## The guns

These are the current numbers. They can change as we balance the game. A player has 20 health (10 hearts), and armor takes some damage away.

| | Pistol | Shotgun | Rifle |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Damage** | 5 (2½ hearts) | 3 per pellet, 6 pellets: up to 18 up close | 8 (4 hearts) |
| **Range** | 40 blocks | 15 blocks | 80 blocks |
| **Fires every** | 0.4 seconds | 1 second | 1.25 seconds |
| **Magazine** | 12 | 6 | 5 |
| **Reload** | 2 seconds | 3 seconds | 3 seconds |
| **Ammo** | Pistol ammo | Shotgun shells | Rifle rounds |

- **Pistol:** cheap, quick, good all round.
- **Shotgun:** the pellets spread out, so it's deadly up close and weak far away.
- **Rifle:** slow, but hits hard from far away.

## The taser

Only guards have one. They get it when they go on duty.

- It does **no damage**. It slows its target right down for **3 seconds**, long enough to [cuff](/wiki/arrests-and-jail) them.
- It reaches **10 blocks**, and recharges in **10 seconds**. It needs no ammo.
- It follows the combat rules: it works on criminals, and on inmates in the prison during a riot.
- It can't be dropped, and it's never sold.

## Losing your guns

When a criminal is arrested, or dies, all their **guns and ammo are taken**. They don't drop for anyone else.

## Without the resource pack

On Java, guns get their look from our resource pack. Without it, they look like plain hoes, but they work exactly the same.
