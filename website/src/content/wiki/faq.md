## Is the server open?

Not yet. Follow the [news](/news) for the launch.

## What version do I need?

Java **26.3**. Newer Java versions can join too. Bedrock: the current version.

## Can Bedrock players play?

Yes, on phone, console and Windows, through Minehut. Same world, same server as Java players. Guns, handcuffs, shops and everything else work the same on both.

## Do I need the resource pack?

Java players get it as they join. It gives guns, handcuffs and keycards their look. If it doesn't load, you can still play: those items look like plain vanilla ones.

## Is it pay to win?

No. The [store](/store) only sells cosmetics: ranks with tags, trails, escape and arrest effects, gun skins and chat colours. Nothing that wins a fight, an escape or a robbery. Guns, ammo, armor and cash are only ever bought or earned in game. See [Ranks and the store](/wiki/ranks-and-store).

## What do I lose when I'm arrested?

Your unbanked cash, your guns and ammo, any keycard, and your bounty. Your bank is safe. See [Arrests and jail](/wiki/arrests-and-jail).

## Are there cars?

No. You get around on foot.

## How do I become a guard?

Apply when applications open. See [Apply](/apply), then [Being a guard](/wiki/guards).

## How do I hide my stats?

Type `/stats private`. You won't show on the website or in Discord. `/stats public` shows you again. See [Commands](/wiki/commands).

## I found a bug or a cheater

Report it. See [Support](/support). Never use a bug: it's a ban.
