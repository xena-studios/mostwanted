## How you get arrested

As a criminal, there are three ways back to prison:

| How | What the guard gets |
| --- | --- |
| A guard **cuffs** you | Your whole bounty |
| A guard **kills** you | Half your bounty |
| You **die any other way** (a fall, lava, drowning) | Nobody gets paid |

Cuffing is one right-click with handcuffs, from up to **3 blocks** away. Guards can't cuff you in a hideout or a safe zone. They **can** cuff you inside the police station, so don't walk in.

## What you lose

- All your **unbanked cash**.
- Your **guns and ammo**. They don't drop: they're gone.
- Any **keycard** you're carrying.
- Your **bounty**, which goes back to zero.

Your **bank** is safe. That's why you bank your cash at a hideout. See [Money and shops](/wiki/money-and-shops).

## Your sentence

You're an **inmate** again, and you serve your sentence in a cell. It's **1 minute, plus 1 minute for every $2,500 of bounty, up to 5 minutes**. These are the current numbers, and they can change.

| Bounty when arrested | Sentence |
| --- | --- |
| Under $2,500 | 1 minute |
| $2,500 | 2 minutes |
| $5,000 | 3 minutes |
| $7,500 | 4 minutes |
| $10,000 or more | 5 minutes |

## In your cell

- **Nothing can hurt you** in a cell, not even lava or a fall.
- Leave the cell and you're **put straight back**.
- Log off, and you're back in your cell when you return. The clock keeps running while you're away.
- You can't shop, go on duty, open gates, break walls or press the riot alarm.

When your time is up, you're let out into the prison. Then it's time to find a way out again. See [Escaping](/wiki/escaping).

## During a riot

Guards can also cuff **inmates** inside the prison while a riot is on. That's a **1-minute** sentence. See [Riots](/wiki/riots).
