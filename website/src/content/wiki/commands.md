Most of the game needs no commands: you walk, punch, right-click and run. Everything else is in the **menu**: right-click the nether star in your last hotbar slot (or tap it in your inventory on Bedrock), or type `/menu`. The commands below open the same menus.

| Command | Who | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/menu` | Everyone | Opens the menu: your profile, settings, the shop, cosmetics, the store, leaderboards and more. |
| `/profile [name]` | Everyone | Your stats, or another player's: all time, this month and this week. |
| `/settings` | Everyone | Turn public stats, the scoreboard, server news and join announcements on or off. |
| `/leaderboards` | Everyone | The leaderboards. Click a player to see their profile. Also `/lb`. |
| `/store` | Everyone | The store: ranks and cosmetics, with a link that opens them in the website's cart. Also `/buy`. |
| `/shop` | Criminals and guards | Opens the shop where you're standing: the hideout shop for criminals in a hideout, the armory for guards at the police station. See [Money and shops](/wiki/money-and-shops). |
| `/duty` | Guards | Goes on duty as a guard, or off duty back to being an inmate. See [Being a guard](/wiki/guards). |
| `/stats` | Everyone | Tells you whether your stats are public or private. |
| `/stats private` | Everyone | Hides you from the website, Discord and the leaderboards. Players in game still see you play. |
| `/stats public` | Everyone | Shows your stats on the website and in Discord again. |
| `/cosmetics` | Everyone | Choose your trail, escape and arrest effects, gun skins and chat colour. See [Ranks and store](/wiki/ranks-and-store). |
| `/joinmessage set <message>` | Legend | Sets the message everyone sees when you join (one line, up to 48 characters). `/joinmessage clear` removes it. |
| `/website` | Everyone | Gives you a one-time code to sign in on [mostwantedmc.com](/login), or to link your Minecraft account there. |

Links to the website, sent in chat: `/rules`, `/wiki` and `/apply`.

## About `/website`

- You type the code on the website, so it works on Bedrock too.
- The code lasts **10 minutes**. Asking for a new one replaces the old one.
- **Never share your code.** Anyone who has it can sign in as you.
