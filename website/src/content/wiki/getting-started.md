## Joining

MostWanted is on **Java and Bedrock**, at `play.mostwantedmc.com`.

- **Java:** Multiplayer → Add Server → paste the address.
- **Bedrock:** the same address. Phone, console and Windows steps go here when the server opens.

> The server isn't open yet. This page is filled in as the game is built.

## Your first minutes

1. You spawn as an **inmate** in your cell block.
2. Look around: the yard, the mess hall, work duty. Anything you pick up might help you escape.
3. When you're ready, find a way out. Read [Escaping](/wiki/escaping).

## The three roles

| Role | How you get it | Goal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Inmate** | Everyone starts here | Escape |
| **Criminal** | Get over the prison wall | Rob the town, stay free |
| **Guard** | By [application](/apply) | Stop escapes, arrest criminals |
