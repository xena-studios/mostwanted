## Joining

MostWanted is on **Java and Bedrock**, at `play.mostwantedmc.com`.

- **Java:** Multiplayer → Add Server → paste the address.
- **Bedrock:** the same address. Phone, console and Windows steps go here when the server opens.

> The server isn't open yet. This page is filled in as the game is built.

On Java, the server sends you our **resource pack** as you join. It gives guns, handcuffs and keycards their look. If it doesn't load, you can still play: those items just look like plain vanilla ones.

## Your first minutes

1. You spawn as an **inmate**, inside the prison.
2. Look for a way out: a weak wall, a gate, the vents. Read [Escaping](/wiki/escaping).
3. Once you're over the wall, you're a **criminal**. Rob places, then bank your cash at a hideout before the guards catch you. Read [Robberies and bounties](/wiki/robberies).

## The three roles

| Role | How you get it | Goal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Inmate** | Everyone starts here | Escape |
| **Criminal** | Get outside the prison | Rob places, bank your cash, stay free |
| **Guard** | By [application](/apply), then `/duty` | Stop escapes, arrest criminals |

Get arrested as a criminal and you're an inmate again. See [Arrests and jail](/wiki/arrests-and-jail).

## Your scoreboard

The scoreboard on the right shows your role and money.

- **Inmates and criminals** see their cash, bank, bounty and wanted stars. If you're serving a sentence, it shows the time left.
- **Guards** see their cash, their arrests, and the bounty they've earned.
- While a robbery or a riot is on, everyone sees a red **HEIST** or **RIOT** section with the place, the time left and how many guards are on duty.
